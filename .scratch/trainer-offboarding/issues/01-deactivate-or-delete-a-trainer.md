# 01 — Offboarding a trainer: deactivate, or delete?

Status: needs-triage

There is no way to remove a trainer's access. No `delete-trainer`, no way to
deactivate an account. A trainer who leaves the club keeps working credentials
indefinitely.

Revoking their sessions ([[pre-deploy-hardening]] 02) does not solve this — they
simply log in again. The gap is at the account level.

## Why this is a feature, not a fix

Split out of [[pre-deploy-hardening]] 02 at triage on 2026-08-04, deliberately,
because the obvious minimal version — "add a `disable-trainer` CLI command" —
quietly pre-decides a domain question that deserves its own discussion.

The question it pre-decides: **must a departed trainer remain visible as the
author of anything?** Today the answer is trivially no. ADR-0001 keeps no trainer
on a `Promotion` — the model records *that* an athlete reached a rank on a date,
never who awarded it — so nothing in the database points at a trainer, and
deleting the row would be consequence-free.

But that is exactly the assumption that breaks if the parked notes-history idea
ever lands: authored, timestamped notes per athlete would make a trainer the
author of records that outlive their membership. Deciding "delete is fine"
now, and building it, is the kind of choice that is cheap today and expensive
after that feature exists.

## What needs deciding

- **Deactivate, delete, or both?** Deactivation keeps the row and refuses login;
  deletion removes it. They are different promises about history, and the answer
  depends on the question above.
- **Is a deactivated trainer still a `Trainer`?** `CONTEXT.md` currently defines a
  Trainer as "a person who logs in to manage athletes and award promotions" — a
  definition by capability, which an account that cannot log in fails. Either the
  glossary entry widens or the state needs its own term.
- **What happens to the athletes they entered?** Nothing, under today's model —
  athletes belong to the club, not to a trainer. Worth confirming rather than
  assuming, because it is the first question anyone will ask.
- **Does offboarding revoke sessions as part of the act?** Almost certainly yes,
  which makes this a caller of the mechanism [[pre-deploy-hardening]] 02 builds
  rather than a duplicate of it.
- **Is there a self-service half?** Probably not — accounts are provisioned
  out-of-band (`CONTEXT.md`), so removal belonging to the operator is consistent.
  Worth stating rather than leaving implied.
- **The last-trainer case.** Deactivating or deleting the only remaining trainer
  locks everyone out of the app permanently. Whether the tool refuses, warns, or
  allows it is a real decision.

## Ordering

**Not a deployment blocker.** The pre-deployment set is the three tickets the
maintainer named plus the hardening items; this is a new feature and gating the
first deployment on it would expand that scope. A club that has not deployed yet
has no departed trainers.

Next step when its time comes: **`/grill-with-docs`**, not `/grill-me` — the
questions above are domain questions whose answers belong in `CONTEXT.md` and
possibly an ADR, and the stateless variant would discard exactly that.
